From bench 🔬 to code 💻

I started my career in the lab, where I quickly realized that I enjoyed analyzing data much more than running experiments. That realization led me to learn programming, and eventually to data science.

I bring both my research background and data science skills to the problems I work on. Research taught me how to ask good questions, dig through the literature, and tackle unfamiliar problems. Data science gave me the tools to explore those questions.
